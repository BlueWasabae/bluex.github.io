Frequently Answered

Institutional Hedging FAQ

Why should our shop hedge when gold generally rises in the long term?

As a gold retailer, your profit engine is the spread between your buying price and selling price plus fabrication markup. You are in the business of high-turnover retail trading, not long-term speculative investing. Sudden 3%–5% intra-week market drops can completely wipe out months of retail profits if your vault inventory remains unhedged.

What happens if gold spikes while we have an open Short hedge?

If the gold spot price surges, your digital short position will show a floating loss. However, the physical gold in your shop's vault appreciates by the exact same dollar amount. That is why holding our recommended Safe Capital Buffer ($100/oz adverse reserve) is essential: it ensures your broker account comfortably absorbs the temporary digital drawdown while your physical bullion gains value.

How do we handle odd weights smaller than 0.01 lot (31.1g)?

Standard MT4/MT5 brokers execute in minimum increments of 0.01 lot (1 Troy Ounce = 31.10g ≈ 0.83 Damlung). For fractional transactions below 1 Troy Ounce, the calculator identifies your exact unhedged micro-residual. You simply accumulate minor retail purchases until reaching the 0.01 lot threshold, maintaining over 98% portfolio hedging efficiency.

Can we hedge other metals like Silver or Platinum?

Yes. The mathematical delta-neutral principles remain identical across Silver (XAG/USD) and Platinum (XPT/USD), adjusting only for standard contract sizes (5,000 oz per standard Silver lot). BlueX Terminal provides custom formulas for multi-metal treasury desks.
