01 Bos iBos • Body-Close Law 📐
Structure Law 01 / 04

Bos iBos: Body-Close Confirmation Over Blind Wicks

Retail traders react to wick rejections and arbitrary trendline breaks, which algorithms routinely engineer as liquidity sweeps. In institutional flow, a true Break of Structure (BOS) requires full candle body closure beyond the swing level. Without a decisive body close, price remains in range consolidation.

“Never predict a break before the close. If the body closes inside the range, the sweep was completed and retail became the liquidity.”
